# PUERTO VALLARTA TRAVEL GUIDE
## June 6-8, 2026

### OVERVIEW
Puerto Vallarta is the ideal June destination with warm weather (88-97°F), fewer tourists, and affordable rates. While occasional afternoon rain is possible, mornings remain sunny for outdoor activities. The city offers an authentic blend of colonial charm and modern amenities.

---

## TOP 5 HIKING ATTRACTIONS

1. **Quimixto Waterfall Hike** - Access via beach access point south of downtown. Moderate 1.5-hour trek through lush tropical forest to a refreshing waterfall and natural pools. Perfect for swimming.

2. **Yelapa Waterfall Trail** - Scenic boat ride to a hidden village followed by a 45-minute waterfall hike. Three-tiered cascade with swimming holes at each level.

3. **Colomitos Beach Hike** - A 3-km coastal trail offering ocean views and pristine, secluded beaches. Excellent combination of hiking and swimming.

4. **Palo María Waterfall** - Local favorite featuring a dramatic 40-meter waterfall. Moderate difficulty, 2-hour round trip through Sierra Madre foothills.

5. **Mirador Conchas Chinas Lookout** - Short 30-minute hike near downtown with panoramic Pacific Ocean views. Perfect for sunset viewing.

---

## BEST SWIMMING BEACHES

- **Los Muertos Beach** - Popular crescent beach near Romantic Zone with calm waters and beach clubs
- **Mismaloya Beach** - Scenic cove with clear water, surrounded by jungle cliffs
- **Conchas Chinas Beach** - Quieter, more upscale area with private beach clubs

## TOP 3 LOCAL RESTAURANTS

1. **Tacos Sonorita Olas Altas** (Budget-Friendly)
   - Authentic street tacos with al pastor and fresh seafood
   - Located: Olas Altas neighborhood
   - Must-try: Fish tacos and shrimp ceviches

2. **Mezcal y Sal** (Mid-Range)
   - Modern Mexican cuisine with regional specialties
   - Beautiful ambiance overlooking the Pacific
   - Famous for ceviche and grilled fish preparations

3. **Pancho's Takos** (Budget-Friendly)
   - Traditional taquería deep in Romantic Zone
   - Local favorite with excellent chorizo and carnitas
   - Authentic, no-frills Mexican dining experience

## CULTURAL TIPS

✓ **Spanish Helps** - While many speak English in tourist areas, learning basic phrases shows respect
✓ **Dress Code** - Casual during day, smart casual for dinner; cover shoulders/knees in cultural sites
✓ **Tipping Culture** - 15-18% standard in restaurants; tip servers and hotel staff
✓ **Water Safety** - Tap water is safe; Puerto Vallarta has award-winning water treatment
✓ **Siesta Time** - Many local shops close 1-4 PM for afternoon rest
✓ **Currency** - Pesos preferred locally; ATMs widely available
✓ **Don't Walk Alone at Night** - Stick to Malecon, Romantic Zone, and tourist areas after dark
✓ **Respect Sacred Sites** - The Cuale Cultural Center and archaeological areas require respectful behavior
✓ **Beach Vendors** - Polite "no gracias" is sufficient; negotiation is expected
✓ **Local Festivals** - June may host small cultural events; ask hotel concierge for current happenings

---

## MUST-SEE ATTRACTIONS

- **Malecon Boardwalk** - 1km scenic promenade with sculptures, restaurants, and sunset views
- **Los Arcos Marine Park** - Iconic natural rock formations; snorkeling and boat tours available
- **Romantic Zone (Viejo Vallarta)** - Charming cobblestone streets, galleries, and colonial architecture
- **Vallarta Botanical Gardens** - 20 acres of tropical gardens with hiking trails and orchid conservatory
- **Cuale River Island & Cultural Center** - Art exhibitions, workshops, and archaeological museum

---

## PRACTICAL TIPS

**Best Times to Hike**: Early morning (6-9 AM) to avoid afternoon heat
**Rainy Season Note**: Bring lightweight rain jacket; rain typically falls 4-6 PM
**Accommodation**: Book near Malecon or Romantic Zone for walkability
**Transportation**: Taxis readily available; use Uber or ask hotel for recommendations
**Bringing**: Sunscreen (SPF 50+), reusable water bottle, insect repellent, waterproof phone case

Puerto Vallarta in June offers authentic Mexican culture, adventurous hiking, excellent seafood, and vibrant nightlife—all with warm hospitality and fewer crowds than peak season.
