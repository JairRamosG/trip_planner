## DAY 2: SATURDAY, JUNE 7, 2026
**Theme: Mountain Hiking & Town Exploration**

**Early Morning (6:00 AM - 10:30 AM)**
**Main Activity: TEPOZTECO NATIONAL PARK & PYRAMID HIKE**
- Challenging 1.5-2 hour ascent to pre-Hispanic pyramid temple at 2,300m elevation
- Panoramic valley views over Tepoztlán canyon
- Early start essential to avoid afternoon rain (June is early rainy season)
- Distance: 3.5 km one-way; elevation gain: 500m
- Trail difficulty: Challenging (steep, rocky sections)
- Bring: Water (3 liters), hiking boots, light rain jacket, sun protection, snacks
- Cost: Free entrance; professional guide available ($200-400 MXN/$12-23 USD)
- Duration including rest: 4-5 hours total

**Important Safety Notes:**
- Start at 6 AM; complete descent by 1 PM before afternoon storms
- Terrain becomes slippery after rain—use caution
- Descent is harder than ascent—go slow and controlled
- No restrooms on trail; use facilities in town before starting

**Afternoon (11:30 AM - 2:00 PM)**
**Lunch Break & Rest**
- Rest at accommodation or café
- Light lunch at CHINAMPA CAFÉ: $80-120 MXN per person ($5-7 USD)

**Late Afternoon (2:30 PM - 5:30 PM)**
**Secondary Activity: TOWN CENTER BIKE TOUR**

- Visit: Artisan shops, 16th-century church, local markets, viewpoints
- Bike rental: $100-150 MXN per bike per day ($6-9 USD)
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Perfect for leisure exploration and authentic cultural immersion

**Evening (6:00 PM - 8:30 PM)**

- Vegetarian options available; modern ambiance with traditional flavors

## DETAILED SAFETY TIPS

**Hiking Safety:**
1. **Altitude Awareness:** Tepozteco reaches 2,300m elevation (7,550 ft). Take breaks every 20-30 minutes. Drink water constantly.
2. **Weather Monitoring:** June is early rainy season. Check weather before 6 AM departure. Afternoon thunderstorms occur 2-4 PM; descend completely by 1 PM.
3. **Trail Conditions:** Rocky, steep sections with exposed drops. Use trekking poles. Wear sturdy hiking boots with good ankle support. Never rush descent.
4. **Sun Exposure:** High altitude intensifies UV rays despite cool temperature (22-28°C). Apply sunscreen every 2 hours; reapply after swimming.
5. **Hydration:** Drink minimum 3 liters water daily given altitude and activity level.
6. **Emergency Contacts:** Save local emergency number (911) in phone. Hike with partner when possible.

**General Safety:**
1. **Night Safety:** Avoid walking alone after dark. Tepoztlán is generally safe but exercise standard precautions.
2. **Valuables:** Use hotel safe for passports and extra cash. Carry only necessary items on hikes.
3. **Water Safety:** Only drink bottled or purified water. Avoid raw vegetables unless from sealed sources if stomach is sensitive.
4. **Medical:** Travel insurance recommended. Nearest hospital: Cuautla (1 hour away). Small clinic in Tepoztlán center for minor issues.
5. **Cultural Respect:** Pyramid is sacred Nahua site. No loud behavior. Ask before photographing locals or rituals.

**Swimming Safety:**
1. **Pool Assessment:** Check water depth before entering. Some pools have underwater rocks.
2. **Current Awareness:** Rivers can swell rapidly after rain. Avoid swimming alone.
3. **Local Knowledge:** Ask locals about safe swimming areas; conditions change seasonally.
