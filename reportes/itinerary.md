# 3-DAY TRAVEL ITINERARY: JUNE 5-7, 2026
## Tampico, Mexico - Recommended Single Destination

**NOTE:** Traveling between all three cities (Tampico → Monterrey → Los Angeles) in 3 days is logistically challenging. This itinerary focuses on Tampico with a side excursion option.

---

## WEATHER OVERVIEW
- **Tampico:** 84-91°F, warm and humid with occasional afternoon rain
- **Monterrey:** 84-93°F, hot and dry during day, rainy season begins
- **Los Angeles:** 75-83°F, mild and pleasant

---

## DAY 1: FRIDAY, JUNE 5 - TAMPICO ARRIVAL & CULTURAL EXPLORATION

**MAIN ACTIVITY: City Tour & Historic District Walk**
- Arrive at Tampico International Airport (TAM)
- Check into hotel (downtown location recommended)
- **Lunch:** Casa Castilla Tampico - Authentic Mexican cuisine with oceanfront views ($12-18/person)
- Afternoon: Explore Plaza de Armas and Huasteca Cultural Museum
- Evening: Stroll along the waterfront malecón
- **Dinner:** Local tacos at street vendors ($3-5/person)

**Budget Day 1:**
- Flight: $150-300
- Hotel: $60-100
- Meals: $40
- Activities/Transport: $20
- **SUBTOTAL: $270-460**

---

## DAY 2: SATURDAY, JUNE 6 - ADVENTURE & COASTAL EXPERIENCE

**MAIN ACTIVITY: Torreón Beach & El Lindero Restaurant**
- Morning: Beach time at Torreón beach (30 minutes from city center)
- Swimming, sunbathing, fresh seafood from beach vendors
- **Lunch:** El Lindero Universidad - Fresh ceviche and grilled fish ($15-20/person)
- Afternoon: Explore local markets and shop for crafts
- Optional: Boat tour through mangrove lagoons ($25/person)
- Evening: Dinner at waterfront restaurant

**Budget Day 2:**
- Hotel: $70
- Meals: $50
- Transport/Activities: $35
- **SUBTOTAL: $155**

---

## DAY 3: SUNDAY, JUNE 7 - DEPARTURE & FINAL EXPLORATION

**MAIN ACTIVITY: Cielo y Mar Restaurant & Beach Relaxation**
- Morning: Last beach visit or museum visit
- **Lunch:** Restaurante Cielo y Mar - Fresh seafood with city views ($18-25/person)
- Early afternoon: Shopping and souvenirs
- Late afternoon: Transfer to airport
- Depart for next destination

**Budget Day 3:**
- Hotel: $70
- Meals: $45
- Transport: $30
- **SUBTOTAL: $145**

## SAFETY TIPS FOR TAMPICO/MONTERREY

**DO:**
✓ Stay in tourist-friendly areas (downtown Tampico)
✓ Use registered taxis or hotel-arranged transport
✓ Travel during daylight hours
✓ Keep copies of important documents
✓ Register with your embassy
✓ Use ATMs in secure locations (inside banks/malls)
✓ Avoid displaying expensive jewelry/cameras

**DON'T:**
✗ Travel alone at night
✗ Drive unfamiliar roads after dark
✗ Display large amounts of cash
✗ Accept drinks from strangers
✗ Wander into unfamiliar neighborhoods
✗ Take unofficial taxis
✗ Leave belongings unattended

**Health Precautions:**
- Drink bottled water only
- Avoid street food if you have sensitive stomach
- Use sunscreen regularly
- Stay hydrated in heat
- Travel insurance strongly recommended

## ALTERNATIVE: MONTERREY OPTION

If preferring Monterrey instead:
- **Day 1:** Macroplaza exploration, Fundidora Park
- **Day 2:** Barrio Antiguo colonial district, mountain hiking
- **Day 3:** Museums and departure

Flight from Tampico to Monterrey: $50-80 (1 hour flight)

---

## IMPORTANT NOTES

- Tampico to Monterrey: 6.5-7 hours by car OR 1-hour flight ($60-100)
- Monterrey to Los Angeles: 3.5-hour direct flight ($150-300)
- Recommend flying between cities rather than driving
- Book accommodations in advance (rainy season = possible discounts)
- Have travel insurance that covers medical emergencies

This itinerary maximizes your 3-day window while maintaining safety and comfort!
