# XALAPA, VERACRUZ: 3-DAY ITINERARY (JUNE 5-7, 2026)

## DAY 1: THURSDAY, JUNE 5, 2026 - ARRIVAL & CULTURAL IMMERSION

**Main Activity:** Museo de Antropología de Xalapa & Historic City Center Exploration

**Detailed Itinerary:**
- 7:00 AM: Depart Cuautla Central Bus Station via ADO first-class coach
- 10:30 AM: Arrive Xalapa; check into budget hotel (Casa Reyes or similar, $20-40 USD/night)
- 12:00 PM: Lunch at **El Comedor de Micaela** - Order traditional mole negro with chicken and fresh tortillas ($7-9 USD per person)
- 2:00 PM: Visit Museo de Antropología (one of Mexico's finest museums featuring Olmec colossal heads and Totonac artifacts; 2-3 hour visit; $4-5 USD entry)
- 5:30 PM: Evening paseo (traditional stroll) on Plaza Xallitic, observing colonial architecture
- 7:00 PM: Dinner at **Café Crespo** - Historic café established 1895; try chilaquiles or regional specialties ($5-7 USD per person)
- 9:00 PM: Rest at hotel

**Day 1 Budget:** Transportation $50-70 (round trip), Hotel $20-40, Meals $22-25, Museum $4-5 = **$96-140 USD**

---

## DAY 2: FRIDAY, JUNE 6, 2026 - MOUNTAIN ADVENTURE

**Main Activity:** Cofre de Perote National Park Cloud Forest Hiking

**Detailed Itinerary:**
- 7:00 AM: Early breakfast at hotel or local bakery ($3-5 USD)
- 8:00 AM: Hire local guide from visitor center ($15-20 USD for group); transport to trailhead
- 9:00 AM-2:00 PM: Moderate 4-hour cloud forest hike through Cofre de Perote featuring misty mountains, waterfalls, and native flora. June's rainy conditions create stunning atmospheric views
- 2:30 PM: Return to Xalapa; lunch at **Casa Xalapa** - Signature xalapeños (poblano peppers stuffed with Oaxaca cheese), fresh agua fresca ($8-10 USD per person)
- 4:00 PM: Visit Jardín Botánico Xalapeño for leisurely 1-hour stroll among mountain gardens and native plant specimens ($2 USD entry)
- 6:00 PM: Rest and freshen up at hotel
- 7:30 PM: Casual dinner near Plaza Xallitic, trying local street food (tlacoyos, gorditas) ($4-6 USD per person)
- 9:00 PM: Return to hotel

**Day 2 Budget:** Meals $18-25, Guide/Transportation $15-20, Museum $2, Tips $5 = **$40-52 USD**

---

## DAY 3: SATURDAY, JUNE 7, 2026 - DEPARTURE & FINAL EXPERIENCES

**Main Activity:** Cascada de Texolo Waterfall Hike & Cultural Market Time

**Detailed Itinerary:**
- 8:00 AM: Breakfast at hotel ($4-6 USD)
- 9:00 AM: Travel to nearby Cascada de Texolo (30-minute drive); 2-3 hour moderate waterfall hike featuring 80-meter dramatic falls ($10-15 USD transport/guide)
- 12:30 PM: Return to Xalapa; final lunch at **Casa Xalapa** or local establishment ($8-10 USD per person)
- 2:00 PM: Explore artisan markets and colonial side streets of historic center; purchase regional crafts, coffee, or mole paste as souvenirs ($10-20 USD optional)
- 4:00 PM: Final coffee and pan dulce (sweet bread) at historic café ($3-5 USD)
- 5:00 PM: Travel to bus station
- 5:45 PM: Depart Xalapa via ADO coach toward Cuautla
- 9:15 PM: Arrive Cuautla Central Station

**Day 3 Budget:** Meals $15-21, Transportation/Guide $10-15, Souvenirs $10-20 (optional), Tips $5 = **$40-61 USD**

## PACKING ESSENTIALS FOR JUNE

- Waterproof jacket (essential for afternoon showers)
- Comfortable hiking boots with good grip
- Light layers (temperature ranges 18-24°C/64-75°F)
- Sun protection (hat, sunscreen SPF 30+)
- Reusable water bottle (stay hydrated at altitude)
- Small daypack for hiking
- Casual clothing for evening activities
- Lightweight quick-dry clothing

---

## SAFETY TIPS

✓ Use official ADO buses; avoid unmarked transport
✓ Avoid displaying valuables; use hotel safes
✓ Stick to main tourist areas at night
✓ Hire certified guides for hiking activities
✓ Stay hydrated due to 1,430m elevation
✓ Purchase travel insurance covering mountain activities
✓ Keep emergency contacts and copy of documents

**Best Travel Value**: Budget-conscious travelers can reduce costs to $180-220 USD by staying in hostels ($15-20/night), eating at local comedores, and sharing guide costs with other travelers.
